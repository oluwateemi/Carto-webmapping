Custom Basemap for Streetlight Mapping FUTA

A custom basemap designed for mapping streetlight accessibility and electricity gaps around the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), Nigeria.

This basemap uses a dark theme to visually reflect the lighting conditions around the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA), Nigeria. Primary roads are rendered in near-white to represent lit corridors, while minor roads and paths appear dim to suggest areas with poor or absent streetlight coverage. The OpenFreeMap vector tileset (OpenMapTiles schema, © OpenStreetMap contributors) is used as the data source, focused on zoom levels 10–18 to keep the map within the campus scale. Buildings and vegetation are rendered in dark tones to avoid competing with the road hierarchy, which is the map's primary focus. To ensure the basemap effectively communicate the intended visual hierarchy, I used dasharray for the roads, having different zone levels functions to reduce cognitive load.

Final Notes

Three custom GeoJSON datasets, all provided under the Open Database License (ODbL), are integrated into the map. These include the FUTA campus boundary, individual streetlight pole locations (originally surveyed by YouthMappersFUTA and contributed to OpenStreetMap), and a 17-metre illumination buffer derived from this OSM data via spatial analysis in QGIS; representing the area each streetlight realistically illuminates along the road network. The high-contrast dark foundation ensures that these thematic layers remain the immediate visual focus, directly reflecting the real experience of navigating a campus with unreliable electricity supply where functional streetlights define the boundary between safe and unsafe movement at night.

  • Base Map Styling

    Two circle styles were used for streetlight markers, one of which includes a blur effect added in Maputnik. The basemap also includes a set center coordinate, zoom level, change glyphs url to tile.OpenFreeMap as the base source, and a custom projection. Place labels were updated to use a free font (Noto Sans) because the default font was not rendering properly on the web.

  • Custom GeoJSON Layers

    Added a custom data source (GeoJSON) layer for the streetlight pole locations, styled with a unique icon and popup information.

  • Interactive Camera Animation and Popup

    I enhanced the experience to allow the basemap to fly to the streetlight location when user clicks on it. I implemented the map.flyTo() method to smoothly animate the camera to a clicked streetlight, while simultaneously triggering a custom-styled popup that dynamically pulls the specific attributes from the GeoJSON properties.
