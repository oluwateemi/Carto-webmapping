Accessibility Map in Lagos Mile 12: Bus Stop Accessibility and Pedestrian Safety.

The project focuses on mapping the accessibility of bus stops and pedestrian safety in the Mile 12 area of Lagos, Nigeria.

Static Map: Google imagery of Lagos Mile 12

This map shows the layout of the Mile 12 area in Lagos, Nigeria, with key location hand coded precisely on the map, using custom made iconography.

Interactive Map: Lagos Mile 12

This interactive map of the Mile 12 area in Lagos, Nigeria, highlights key locations such as the Mile 12 Market, Bus Terminal, and Pedestrian Bridge. The map uses custom SVG icons for clear visual representation and includes tooltips for each point of interest. Additionally, a highlighted rectangle indicates the central market area, providing users with an engaging way to explore accessibility and safety features in this bustling urban environment.

Final Notes

  • Responsive Overlays:

    Used percentage-based absolute positioning and CSS transforms to ensure static map markers remain geographically accurate on all screen sizes.

  • Custom Iconography:

    Designed and implemented three distinct custom icon sets for various infrastructure types (Transit, Pedestrian, Market).

  • Interactive Feedback:

    Integrated CSS hover effects on markers and centered the map view to the location of the area of interest.
